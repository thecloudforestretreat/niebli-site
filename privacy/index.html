Privacy and analytics

Niebli uses optional Google Analytics to understand how visitors discover and explore its stories. Analytics loads only when you allow it using Analytics preferences. You can decline or change your choice at any time using the button on each page.

When enabled, Google Analytics receives page visits, device and browser information, broad geographic information, traffic sources, campaign information, and interactions with content. It uses cookies to distinguish visits and returning browsers. We do not deliberately send form contents, contact details, private family information, or visitor names to Analytics. Advertising storage and advertising personalization remain disabled.

Your preference is stored in this browser. Declining stops new analytics collection; it does not delete data previously collected. Browser restrictions, consent choices, and blockers can affect the completeness of reports.

Learn about Google’s use of information from partner sites and the Google Analytics opt-out tool.
